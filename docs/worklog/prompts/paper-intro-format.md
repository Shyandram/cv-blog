# Owner's format for a paper introduction (2026-11-01)
Goal: after reading, one understands every function of the design, the challenge, the method, the results and ablations, the conclusion and the open problems.
Sections, in order: Challenge · Method (every component: what it does, why it is there, how it works precisely — equations with symbols defined) · Results (datasets, metrics, settings, numbers as printed) · Ablations (each study: change, effect, what it proves) · Conclusion · Problems (limitations, caveats, what later work did).
Not the "reading guide" form (how to read its tables / read it yourself). Detailed, not short.
